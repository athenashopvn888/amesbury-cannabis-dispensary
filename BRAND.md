# Amesbury brand system

- Primary: `#063B2B` deep emerald. Used for large backgrounds and brand surfaces.
- Accent: `#D5A52E` warm metallic gold. Used for rules, active states and restrained highlights.
- Supporting green: `#5DBB38`. Used sparingly for botanical detail and positive emphasis.
- Dark text: `#112018`; light text: `#FFFDF3`.

The identity is derived from the supplied green-and-gold storefront while using an original layout and typography system. White on deep emerald and dark text on the cream surface provide strong reading contrast. Gold is not used for small body copy on light backgrounds.

Generated botanical art is illustrative campaign artwork. It is not presented as a photograph of the store, products, staff, customers or a local landmark.
